welcome to the patch

This is a real hollow 3D pumpkin, wearing a doodle shader.

  • Drag to rotate; scroll to zoom. “Front view” resets the camera.
  • Choose a category, then a part. “None” removes it. Left and right features can be linked or edited separately.
  • Move, scale and rotate each feature. Teeth grow from the mouth rim; change their size and spacing under Teeth.
  • “Freehand carver” locks the front view. Draw a closed shape and release to make an opening.
  • “Light it up” lifts the lid, lowers a candle, and puts the lid back. Candlelight always keeps the doodle style.
  • Save to the gallery or an editable .pumpkin file. GLB export contains closed, separate body and lid meshes in metres.
  • Carving guides download as printable HTML. Open in your browser and print at 100%. Large patterns state their reduction scale.
  • For 3D printing, import the GLB into your slicer or convert it to STL. Check the slicer’s mesh report; thin details may need enlargement.

Keyboard: Ctrl/⌘ Z to undo, Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z to redo, Escape to exit a tool.
